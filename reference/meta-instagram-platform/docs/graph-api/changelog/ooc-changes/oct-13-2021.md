# October 13, 2021

## Reach Estimate

We understand that the shift to ranges may be challenging for some to implement by October 27, 2021. As such, through November 15, we will continue to make available a single-point estimate as an additional endpoint.

The following changes apply to *potential reach* for all versions:

* The name of *potential reach* is changing to *estimated audience size*. Estimated audience size generally estimates how many people meet the targeting and ad placement criteria that advertisers select while creating an ad. [Learn more about reach estimate](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-estimate/).
* The definition of the [`reachestimate` API](/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/reach-estimate) has changed from *Get an estimated number of individuals for your targeting and what bids to make to reach that target group with the /reachestimate API* to *Get a range of individuals for your targeting and what bids to make to reach that target group with the /reachestimate API*.
* The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/reachestimate`](/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/reach-estimate) endpoint has two new fields: `users_lower_bound` and `users_upper_bound`.
* On October 27, 2021, the `users` field will be deprecated.

## Delivery Estimate

The following changes apply to *delivery estimate* for all versions:

* The [`GET /{adset-id}/delivery_estimate`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-campaign-delivery-estimate/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.
* The [`GET /{ad-account-id}/delivery_estimate`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-campaign-delivery-estimate/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.
* The [`GET /{ad-account-id}/adsets`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-account/adsets/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.
* On October 27, 2021, the `estimate_mau` field will be deprecated.

## Reach & Frequency

The following changes apply to reach & frequency for all versions and only applied for predictions created after October 29, 2021 onward. Old predictions won't show any size after November 15, 2021.

* The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/reachfrequencypredictions`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-frequency-prediction/) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.
* The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/ReachFrequencyPrediction`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-frequency-prediction/) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.
* On October 27, 2021, the `target_audience_size` field will be deprecated.

## Custom Broad Category Targeting

The following changes apply to *custom broad category targeting* for all versions:

* The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/broadtargetingcategories`](/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/advanced-targeting) endpoint has two new fields: `size_lower_bound` and `size_upper_bound`. * The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/customaudiences`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience) endpoint has two new fields: `approximate_count_lower_bound` and `approximate_count_upper_bound`.
* The [`GET /savedaudience`](/docs/marketing-api/reference/saved-audience/) endpoint has two new fields: `approximate_count_lower_bound` and `approximate_count_upper_bound`.
