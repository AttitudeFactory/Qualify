# Data Handling Questions



Starting on or after June 12th, 2023, if your app requires advanced level access to permissions or features, you may need to complete data handling questions. See this [blog post](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2023/04/05/data-handling-questions/) for more information.

Beginning January 2024, developers with apps that have been published live with a use case or advanced access to any [permission](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/permissions/reference) or [feature](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/features-reference) will be required to complete [data handling questions](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/release/data-handling-questions/questions-preview) as part of their annual [Data Use Checkup](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/data-use-checkup).

## Overview

Completing our data handling questions is a requirement for:

* Apps that require access to use cases, [permissions](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/permissions/reference) or [features](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/features-reference) (for some apps this is called [advanced access](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/overview/access-levels#advanced-access)).
* Apps that have been published live with a use case, or that have advanced access to permissions or features. This must be completed annually as part of the Data Use Checkup (DUC).

These questions are designed to validate that personal data received from Meta will be processed and transferred securely and in compliance with [Meta’s Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and [Developer Policies](https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy). This is part of Meta’s continued commitment to protecting people’s privacy and personal data—a responsibility that we share with all developers in our ecosystem.

It is recommended that you consult with legal, policy, and privacy experts within your organization for guidance on how to answer these questions.
