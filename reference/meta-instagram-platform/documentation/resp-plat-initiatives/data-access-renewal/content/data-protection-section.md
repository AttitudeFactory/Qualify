# Data Protection Section



The following questions are part of the data protection section of data access renewal. Please note that the question numbers have changed from the individual assessment versions (e.g. 3.1-1 is now practices-1).

Instructions for use:

We recommend that app administrators review these with their internal teams as a way to ensure apps meet the requirements of our Platform Terms. All app administrators will be notified if the data protection section is required for each app.

You may need to upload documentation that shows how and why this Platform Data is being used. This documentation must be either written in English or accompanied by an English translation

These questions are displayed here for your convenience only. The questions required for a given app will vary based on the data to which each app has access. If an app has access to certain types of data, you may also need to provide evidence to support your answers.

If you are currently in the process of completing data protection questions, or are addressing follow-up questions from our reviewers, please continue with those processes and note that the questions you are currently answering may be different from the updated questions here.
