# LinkedIn bitácora — rotating theme bank

English themes for the daily FDE / neuroscience-coaching journal. The `linkedin-bitacora` agent pulls one theme per weekday that hasn't been used recently (check `bitacora/log.md`), and blends it with the day's abstracted work. All themes must be tellable **without revealing anything about the product** — they're lenses on the *human and engineering craft*, not the build.

## FDE craft & mindset
- Build vs. buy: the discipline of not building the thing that isn't your edge.
- Shipping inside ambiguity: making a call with 60% of the information.
- Cutting scope as a superpower — the feature you *didn't* build.
- Translating a messy real-world problem into a system that holds.
- The forward-deployed mindset: engineering *with* the customer's reality, not at arm's length.
- Idempotency & doing-things-twice-safely as a life metaphor.
- Reversible vs. irreversible decisions — and spending your worry budget accordingly.
- Prototypes that ship vs. notebooks that rot.
- Reading the primary source instead of the summary — in code and in life.

## AI-engineering lessons (all generic, no specifics)
- Evaluations before optimism: why "it looks like it works" isn't done.
- The cost of a wrong abstraction you can't change later.
- Latency/cost/quality is a triangle; you only get to keep two.
- When "smaller and boring" beats "bigger and impressive."
- Debugging as hypothesis-testing, not flailing.
- The gap between a demo and a system that runs unattended.

## Founder / operator
- Solo-building: being your own PM, engineer, and second brain.
- Momentum over motivation — systems that make the next step obvious.
- Saying no to good ideas to protect the one that matters.
- Working in public without giving away the store.

## Neuroscience & coaching bridge (aim ~1 in 3 posts)

- Habit loops (cue → routine → reward) applied to a coding practice.
- The brain under stress narrows — how to widen it before a big decision.
- Deep work and the neuroscience of attention residue / context-switching cost.
- Dopamine and the trap of shipping for the hit vs. the outcome.
- Neuroplasticity: why career change is biologically possible at any age.

- Career transformation as a rewiring problem, not a willpower problem.
